Vedium Referral Program

Refer friends to Vedium and you both win: your friend gets an automatic discount on enrollment, and you get a reward coupon as soon as their enrollment is confirmed. Everything is tracked in a dashboard inside the platform.

  • Every logged-in student has their own referral code, generated automatically.
  • Your friend's discount is applied directly at checkout, with no manual approval needed.
  • Your reward arrives by email as soon as enrollment is confirmed, with the coupon ready to use.

How it works

Automatic referrals, inside the platform

Flow

You go to "My referrals" on the platform and share your code with a friend. When they enroll using your code, the discount is applied automatically at their checkout, and your reward is generated and emailed to you — no waiting.

The full dashboard, with your code and referral history, lives under "My referrals" in the logged-in area.

1

Get your code

Log in, go to "My referrals" and share your unique link with friends.

2

Automatic discount

Your friend uses the code at checkout and the discount applies instantly, with no manual approval.

3

Reward by email

As soon as enrollment is confirmed, you automatically receive a reward coupon.

Important rules

Each referred person generates a reward only once for the referrer, even if they use the same code again on another purchase.
Nobody can use their own referral code.
The reward coupon is valid for 180 days from issuance.
