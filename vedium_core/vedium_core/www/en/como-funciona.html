Before you enroll

You understand the path before you pay

Vedium keeps the public website separate from the LMS platform. The website helps you choose your track, get your questions answered and complete the initial diagnostic. Enrollment and class access happen on the secure platform at app.vediums.com.

  • Placement test: estimates CEFR level, goal and an initial recommendation.
  • Support: confirms language, timeline, availability and the best plan.
  • Plan: defines frequency, level, goal and study pace.
  • Payment: made on the platform, with Stripe and clear rules in our Terms.
  • Classes: live sessions, guided practice, assignments and progress by track.
What defines your track

Not a one-size-fits-all course

Current level

Vedium considers whether you're at A1, A2, B1, B2 or C1, and avoids placing you in a class that doesn't match your foundation.

Goal

Job interviews, travel, tech, business, Portuguese for foreigners and Yoruba all require different vocabulary and practice.

Timeline

If you have an interview or trip coming up, you need focused, intensive study. If you're aiming for fluency, you can follow a fuller progression.

Frequency

Plans with more classes per week speed up exposure, correction and speaking practice. The recommendation depends on your routine.

Format

Classes are live, online and led by a real teacher. You're never left alone watching a sequence of recorded videos.

Progress tracking

Progress accounts for participation, speaking, assignments, vocabulary and real progress within your chosen track.

Questions before you start

Frequently asked questions

Is the placement test mandatory?

It doesn't replace a full assessment, but it's the best first step to guide the conversation with our team and avoid picking the wrong level.

Can I talk to someone before paying?

Yes. The WhatsApp CTAs exist specifically to answer questions about language, plan, frequency and track before you enroll.

Does the website enroll me automatically?

No. The public website informs you and captures interest. Enrollment, payment and course access happen on the LMS platform.

Does Vedium sell to students outside Brazil?

Yes. The website now has a language selector and can run international campaigns. Our team can guide foreign students interested in Portuguese, English or Yoruba.
