FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Before you choose your course

About Vedium

Are classes live or pre-recorded?

Our main classes are live and online, with a teacher. Support materials may be used between sessions.

How do I know which level to choose?

Start with the free placement test. Afterward, our team can guide you to the track that best fits your goal. See more in our methodology.

Which languages does Vedium offer?

Our public catalog features English, Yoruba and Portuguese for foreigners, with tracks by level and goal.

Is there a long-term commitment?

Plans are monthly, with no long-term contract. Cancellation and refund terms are detailed in our Terms of Use.

How long does each class last?

Live classes last 1 hour, with a teacher correcting pronunciation and sentence construction in real time.

English

Which levels does the English course cover?

From A1 to C1, with the class defined by the result of the free placement test.

Is it suitable for someone who already has some English?

Yes. The placement test identifies whether you're at A2, B1, B2 or C1 and avoids putting you in a class that doesn't fit.

Yoruba

Do I need to know any Yoruba before starting?

No. The course starts from scratch, with the alphabet, tones and greetings, and builds up with cultural depth, not just isolated vocabulary.

Does the Yoruba course cover culture, or just the language?

Both together. At Vedium, Yoruba is taught with cultural context, songs and greetings — see more in our Yoruba course.

Portuguese for foreigners

Is Portuguese for foreigners suitable for any level?

Yes, from beginner to advanced, focused on real situations for people who live, study or work in Brazil.

Is it suitable for someone who already lives in Brazil?

Yes, the course is designed both for newcomers and for those already living here who want to build fluency.

Payment and plans

Does paying change my access automatically?

Checkout and the LMS platform live at app.vediums.com. This page is informational only and doesn't affect billing.

Can I switch plans after subscribing?

Yes, talk to our team via contact or WhatsApp to adjust your plan as you progress.

How does cancellation work?

There is no long-term contract. Full conditions are detailed on our cancellation and refund page.

Does Vedium issue a certificate?

Yes, for courses completed according to their requirements. It's a free-course certificate, verifiable on our certificate page.
