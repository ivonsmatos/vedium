How to decide

The right plan is the one you can keep up

The best frequency isn't necessarily the most intense one. It needs to fit your routine, keep you consistent, allow time for review, and give you enough practice for your timeline.

  • Professional English: 2 or more classes per week when you have an upcoming interview, meeting or presentation.
  • Portuguese for foreigners: 2 classes per week help with everyday adaptation, conversation and cultural confidence.
  • Yoruba: a regular frequency makes pronunciation, memory, greetings, structure and cultural context easier to absorb.
  • Celpe-Bras: usually needs a more focused plan, with writing, speaking and mock exams.
