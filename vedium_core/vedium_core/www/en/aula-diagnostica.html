Diagnostic Class

Before you enroll

Confirm your level with human guidance

The free test gives you a CEFR estimate. The diagnostic class helps turn that result into a practical decision: recommended level, goal, frequency, best track and next steps at Vedium.

It's a great fit if you want to avoid enrolling at the wrong level, have a specific deadline, are coming from another school, or need Portuguese for foreigners, professional English or Yoruba with cultural context.

What our team reviews

  • Placement test result and open-ended answers.
  • Goal: career, interview, travel, Celpe-Bras, culture, customer service, or general fluency.
  • Weekly availability and timeline to progress.
  • Main challenges: speaking, listening, writing, grammar, vocabulary or confidence.
  • Recommended track, plan and next step.
How it works
