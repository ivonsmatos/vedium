Why Vedium

What really changes when you learn a language here

Vedium was built to solve a common problem: courses that promise fluency but deliver pre-recorded video, tight deadlines and cancellation fees. Here it's different.

100% live classes

No pre-recorded video as the main lesson. You get a real teacher, in real time, correcting your pronunciation and answering questions on the spot — the way you actually learn to speak a language.

No long-term contract

Plans are monthly. You stay because you're progressing, not because you're locked into a cancellation fee. Cancel anytime, in line with our Terms of Use.

Qualified teachers

Each track has a teacher who specializes in the language and its culture — including Yoruba, taught with real cultural depth, not as a decorative curiosity.

Completion certificate

Once you complete the course according to the requirements, you receive a free-course completion certificate, verifiable at vediums.com/en/certificado.

Free placement test

Before signing up for any plan, find out your real level with the free placement test — no strings attached.

Real support

Questions about your class, payment or the platform? Talk directly to our team on WhatsApp, no chatbot making you repeat the question five times.
