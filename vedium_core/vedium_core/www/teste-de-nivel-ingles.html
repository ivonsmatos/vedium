Parte 2

Reading comprehension

Text 1: Lucas's new job

Lucas started a new job at an international company. Every morning, he reads short emails in English and joins a quick meeting with his team. At first, he was nervous, but now he understands the main ideas and asks simple questions when he needs help.

11. What does Lucas do every morning?

12. In English, write one sentence explaining how Lucas felt at first.

Text 2: hybrid work

Hybrid work has reshaped how companies organize collaboration. While employees often appreciate flexibility, managers must intentionally create routines that preserve communication, accountability and a shared sense of purpose across distributed teams.

13. What is the main idea of Text 2?

14. In the text, "preserve communication" is closest in meaning to:

15. Explain, in your own words, what "a shared sense of purpose" means.
